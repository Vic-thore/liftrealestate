How we work

Every project moves through four stages. You know where things stand, what comes next, and what it costs — at every point.

01

Consultation

We visit the site, review your brief and budget, and assess zoning and feasibility before any design work begins.

02

Concept Design

Sketches, massing studies, and material direction. You review two to three concepts and we refine one together.

03

Development & Documentation

The approved concept becomes detailed drawings — structural coordination, permits, and tender-ready documents.

04

Construction & Handover

We stay on through the build: site visits, contractor queries, and quality checks until the keys are in your hand.
